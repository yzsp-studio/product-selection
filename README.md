## 🇨🇳 中文

电商选品与品类机会分析技能：根据商家的预算、目标平台和货源优势，按「市场需求×竞争度×利润空间×季节性×复购率」五维打分，输出 3-5 个候选品类及切入建议。适用于新手不知道卖什么、老商家想拓新品类、以及想判断某个品值不值得进货的场景。技能内写明了适用场景、使用方法、输入信息、分析框架、输出格式与注意事项，按框架走就能得到可比的候选结果。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入预算/目标平台/货源优势 → 输出 3-5 个候选品类
- 五维打分：市场需求×竞争度×利润空间×季节性×复购率
- 每个候选品类附具体切入建议
- 覆盖三类场景：新手选品、老商家拓品类、单品进货判断
- 内置分析框架与输出格式，结果可横向比较

## 🇬🇧 English

An e-commerce product-selection and category-opportunity skill: given a merchant's budget, target platform and sourcing advantages, it scores candidates across five dimensions, market demand, competition, profit margin, seasonality and repurchase rate, then outputs 3-5 candidate categories with entry recommendations. It fits newcomers who do not know what to sell, established sellers exploring new categories, and anyone judging whether a specific product is worth stocking. The skill documents when to use it, how to use it, what inputs it needs, its analysis framework, its output format and its cautions.

### ✨ What it does

- Budget, target platform and sourcing edge in, 3-5 categories out
- Five-dimension scoring: demand, competition, margin, seasonality, repurchase
- Each candidate category comes with a concrete entry recommendation
- Covers newcomers, sellers adding categories and single-product stock calls
- Built-in framework and output format keep candidates comparable
